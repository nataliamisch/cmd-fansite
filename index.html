A brief intro to Skales

So, who exactly is Skales?
Skales is a character from the animated lego show titled Lego Ninjago Masters of Spinjitzu. He is a Hypnobrai serpentine (one of the five established tribes in the universe of Ninjago). Hypnobrai appear as blue scaled serpentine that have the ability to hypnotise using their (often) red eyes. Originally, he was a secondary villain in the duration of the first two seasons, however after season 3 he reforms and becomes a reccuring side-character and ally to the ninja main protagonists. During his time spent as a villain, he falls into the starscream-esque archetype of a disloyal second-in-command to those he serves.

He starts off as a warrior, which we do not see during the shows run, later he becomes second-in-command to his general, Slithraa. When in episode 1 of season 1, the general winds up accidentally hypnotising himself and starts following the will of a young human boy named Lloyd Garmadon, Skales decides he should take action and challenges his general for his title. He does so in a slither-pit (soundtrack can be found at bottom of page). A slither-pit being a fighting-ring from serpentine culture. He wins this fight and takes the title of general.
Later that season he allies with the main antagonist, Pythor P. Chumsworth, who grants him a position as his right hand man, if Skales is to help him become king of all serpentine kind. Eventually when Pythor is rendered defeated and persumably dead by the end of the season, Skales decides its his responsibility to lead. Unfortunately, his attempt to convince his people is fruitless and Lord Garmadon (the second season's main antagonist) convinces the serpentine to follow him instead. Thus, Skales spends the majority of the season winning the support of the serpentine back.
When he does achieve this and manage to rid of Lord Garamdon, he spends one episode as king of the serpentine, where he calls an order to burrow beneath Ninjago's capital, Ninjago City, to destroy it from the bottom up. Though this ends catastrophically when during their digging they stumble upon a tomb of unbreakable stone warriors that come alive and leave them trapped underground. This marks the end of Skales' prescence as a villain and (until the release of the reboot 2 years later) is the end for his character as a whole.

The Ninjago wiki descibes his personality as cunning and ambitious, willing to whatever it takes to get his way, but more importantly, to do what he finds right for his own kind. He also descibed as being somewhat cowardly, but on occasion showing moments of bravery.

Where my interest started

I first started watching Lego Ninjago back in 2017, when I was merely a 8 year old child. I quickly grew attached to the show, as well as many of the characters, one of these being Skales himself. The fixation at first started more like a weird 'rivalrly' if anything. I would imagine my made up ninja character fighting against Skales and the other serpentine and make up all the scenarios with him as the villain in said stories. However slowly that'd change and he would 'reform' himself in the stories so to speak.
